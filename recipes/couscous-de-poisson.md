## Notes
White fish and salmon steamed above a spiced vegetable and chickpea base (carrots, courgettes, tomatoes, ras el hanout), served with fluffed couscous — written for a multicooker (Companion/Cookeo-style) with pressure/steam programme steps. Full ingredients and method (in French) at the source link above.
