## Ingredients
- 3 eggs
- 150 g flour
- 1 sachet baking powder
- 100 ml sunflower oil
- 125 ml whole milk
- 100 g grated gruyère
- 200 g smoked lardons
- 80 g pitted prunes
- 1 pinch salt
- 2 pinches pepper

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (gas mark 6).
2. In a mixing bowl, whisk together the eggs, flour, baking powder, salt and pepper. Gradually incorporate the oil and the pre-warmed milk. Add the grated gruyère and mix.
3. Fry the lardons without any added fat for 5 minutes. Cut the prunes into pieces and mix them with the lardons.
4. Fold the lardon and prune mixture into the batter and mix.
5. Pour into an ungreased loaf tin and bake for 45 minutes.

## Notes
A classic French savoury loaf cake, salty-sweet from the smoked bacon and prunes — good for an apéro or a picnic slice.
