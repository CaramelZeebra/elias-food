## Notes
Layers of roasted Crown Prince pumpkin (or butternut squash), a celery-and-tomato sauce, nutmeg-buttered spinach and a peppercorn-infused béchamel, built up with fresh lasagne sheets and baked until golden. Recipe from Good Food magazine, December 2009. Full ingredients and method at the source link above.
