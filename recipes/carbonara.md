## Ingredients
- 200 g spaghetti
- 100 g lardons, diced
- 4 egg yolk
- 100 g parmesan, grated
- 1 tonne black pepper

## Instructions
1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the spaghetti until al dente.
2. While the pasta cooks, fry the lardons in a cold pan over medium heat until crisp and golden.
3. Whisk the parmesan and yolks together in a bowl with a generous amount of black pepper.
4. Drain the pasta, reserving a cup of pasta water, and add it straight into the guanciale pan off the heat.
5. Quickly stir in the egg mixture, adding splashes of pasta water until glossy and coating the pasta.
6. Serve immediately with extra parmesan and pepper.

## Notes
Never let the eggs hit direct heat or you'll end up with scrambled egg pasta. Off-heat and quick stirring is the whole trick.
