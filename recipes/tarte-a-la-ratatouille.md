## Notes
A shortcrust tart base blind-baked, then topped with a frozen ratatouille reduced down with fresh tomato and herbs, scattered with chunks of goat's tomme and finished with a savoury egg custard baked until golden. Good served warm as a starter or cold in small squares as a nibble. Full ingredients and method (in French) at the source link above.
