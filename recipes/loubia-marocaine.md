## Ingredients
- 250 g dried white beans, soaked overnight
- 300 g beef
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 tomatoes, crushed (or 200ml tomato purée)
- 2 tsp tomato concentrate
- 2 carrots, sliced
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 garlic clove (or 1 tsp garlic powder)
- 1 tbsp ground ginger
- 1/2 tsp cumin
- 1 bay leaf
- salt and pepper
- water

## Instructions
1. Cut the meat into small pieces. Peel and chop the onion.
2. In a cocotte (Dutch oven), brown the meat, onion, spices and bay leaf for 15 to 20 minutes, stirring regularly. Add a splash of water if needed to stop it catching.
3. Add the tomato purée, tomato concentrate and carrot slices, and mix.
4. Add the drained beans and water — 2 volumes of water for 1 volume of beans — and adjust the seasoning.
5. Close the pressure cooker and cook under pressure for 20 minutes. Without a pressure cooker, cooking will take about 1 hour (or more).
6. Serve hot with good homemade Moroccan bread.

## Notes
Prep: 15 min. Cook: 45 min. Total: 60 min.
