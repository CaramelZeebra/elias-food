## Ingredients
- 225 g flour
- 3 eggs
- 100 g butter
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 2/3 glass milk
- 20 g yeast
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 litre water (for the syrup)
- 250 g sugar (for the syrup)
- 5 tbsp rum

## Instructions
1. Bring all the base ingredients (eggs, butter, milk) to room temperature.
2. Dissolve the yeast in the milk, just lukewarm and sweetened.
3. In a warm bowl, put in the flour and make a well in the centre.
4. Add the salt and the beaten eggs.
5. Mix in, gradually adding the yeast.
6. Work in the melted, cooled butter.
7. Work the dough by hand for ten minutes — it should be quite soft and runny.
8. Cover the bowl with a floured cloth and leave to rise somewhere warm for at least two hours, until it doubles in size.
9. Gently knock the dough back down.
10. Put it into a well-buttered 25cm-diameter mould and leave to rest for an hour.
11. Once the mould is about three-quarters full, bake (gas mark 6.5, 25-30 min) until the cake is dry and golden.
12. Turn it out of the mould while still warm and douse it with hot rum syrup.
13. For the syrup: heat the water and sugar, then add the rum off the heat just before it boils.
14. Serve with whipped cream or pastry cream.

## Notes
Rum in the syrup can go up to 6 tablespoons if you want it stronger.
