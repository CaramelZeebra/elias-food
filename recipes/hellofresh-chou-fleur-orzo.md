## Ingredients
- 2 cauliflower
- 1.5 sachet dried oregano
- 300 g orzo
- 2 onion
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1 Parmigiano Reggiano AOP
- 2 packets crushed tomatoes
- 2 sachets grated mozzarella
- 2 pots basil cream
- 6 tbsp olive oil
- 200 ml vegetable stock
- 2 tsp sugar
- salt and pepper, to taste

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Cut the cauliflower into florets and the stalks into pieces. Place the cauliflower in an oven dish, drizzle with a little olive oil and 1/2 tsp dried oregano per person. Season with salt and pepper. Roast for 20 min, until the cauliflower is well browned and slightly tender.
2. Make the stock with hot water. Bring a pan of water to the boil for the orzo. Cook for 10–12 min, or until tender, then drain and set aside, covered.
3. Finely chop the onion and garlic. Grate the parmesan. Heat a drizzle of olive oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat. Sauté the onion and garlic for 3–4 min. Add the crushed tomatoes, the stock, and per person: 1/2 tsp dried oregano and 1/2 tsp sugar. Season lightly with salt and pepper. Simmer for 3–4 min, or until the sauce has reduced slightly.
4. After 20 min of roasting the cauliflower, carefully pour half the tomato sauce over it. Sprinkle with grated mozzarella and half the parmesan. Continue roasting for a further 10–15 min on a high shelf, until the cauliflower is tender and the cheese is golden.
5. Meanwhile, in the pan with the remaining tomato sauce, add the orzo, half the basil cream and the rest of the parmesan. Mix well, season with salt and pepper. Reheat for 2–3 min over medium heat.
6. Serve the orzo in bowls. Top with the gratinated cauliflower. Drizzle with basil cream.
