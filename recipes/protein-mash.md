## Ingredients
- 3 large potatoes
- 30 g vanilla protein powder
- 1 cup milk
- 50 g red cheddar
- spices

## Instructions
1. Peel, dice, and boil potatoes until they get fluffy and fall off the fork.
2. Drain and add other ingredients.
3. Mash

## Notes
This recipe is a little controversial, but worth trying for the protein lovers out there.
