## Notes
Veggie Moroccan-style stew — roasted new potatoes, fennel, carrot, pepper and red onion combined with a chickpea, tomato, red wine, orange and prune sauce. Serve over couscous with toasted flaked almonds. Recipe originally from Good Food magazine, May 2010.
