## Ingredients
- 1 kg chestnuts
- 1 onion
- 1 leek
- 1 celery stalk
- 20 g butter
- 1 l chicken stock
- 250 ml crème fraîche

## Instructions
1. Finely slice the onion, leek and celery, and let them soften gently in the butter for about 5 minutes.
2. Stir in the chestnuts along with the chicken stock, and leave to cook for around 5 minutes.
3. Finish by stirring through the crème fraîche.

## Notes
For 6 people. A short, simple French winter soup — good blended smooth or left a little chunky, to taste.
