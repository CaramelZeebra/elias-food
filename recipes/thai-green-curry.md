## Ingredients
- 400 ml coconut milk
- 3 tbsp green curry paste
- 500 g chicken thigh, sliced
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 100 g green beans, trimmed
- 2 kaffir lime leaves
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tsp palm sugar
- 1 handful thai basil

## Instructions
1. Simmer half the coconut milk with the curry paste in a pan until fragrant and the oil separates.
2. Add the chicken and cook through, stirring occasionally.
3. Pour in the rest of the coconut milk, lime leaves, fish sauce, and palm sugar.
4. Add the bell pepper and green beans, simmer until just tender.
5. Stir through the thai basil at the end and serve with jasmine rice.
