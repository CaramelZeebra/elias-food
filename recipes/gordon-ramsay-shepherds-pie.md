## Notes
A richer take on shepherd's pie: minced lamb with grated onion and carrot, deglazed with red wine and simmered with Worcestershire sauce and herbs, topped with riced mash enriched with egg yolks and Parmesan, served alongside pan-roasted carrots. Full ingredients and method at the source link above.
