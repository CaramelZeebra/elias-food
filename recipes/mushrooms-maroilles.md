## Ingredients
- 16 large mushrooms (or more)
- 20 cl liquid cream
- 1/2 maroilles (about 200 g), plus a little extra for topping
- 100 g grated gruyère
- salt, pepper

## Instructions
1. Melt the cream with the maroilles and the gruyère over low heat.
2. Put the mushroom caps in an ovenproof dish and salt them lightly.
3. Fill them with the maroilles cream.
4. Bake for 20 minutes at 180°C.
5. Remove any excess liquid that's pooled in the dish.
6. Place a small piece of maroilles on each mushroom and grill until golden.
