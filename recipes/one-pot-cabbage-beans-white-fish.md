## Notes
Based on garbure, a peasant dish from south-west France — bacon, cabbage and flageolet beans simmered in white wine and stock, topped with pan-fried skin-on white fish fillets. Recipe from Good Food magazine, March 2009. Full ingredients and method at the source link above.
