## Notes
Whole roasted butternut squash and slow-cooked frozen spinach rolled up in fresh pasta sheets with feta, sliced and baked in a garlicky passata sauce, finished with Parmesan and crispy edges. From Jamie's Save With Jamie / Keep Cooking and Carry On. Full ingredients and method at the source link above.
