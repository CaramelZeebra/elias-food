## Notes
Leftover cooked lamb and roasted cauliflower layered with parboiled basmati rice and saffron-infused milk, baked in foil, then finished with a crispy cashew, mint and chilli topping and a quick carrot raita. From Jamie: Keep Cooking and Carry On. Full ingredients and method at the source link above.
