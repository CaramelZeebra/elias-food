## Notes
Diced lamb shoulder browned then layered with softened onion and garlic, topped with alternating rounds of courgette and tomato, drizzled with olive oil and thyme, and baked until the vegetables are tender and lightly caramelised. Full ingredients and method (in French) at the source link above.
