## Ingredients
- 1 ripe avocado
- 350 g mixed-colour cherry tomatoes
- 100 g baby spinach
- 3 spring onions
- 1/2 bunch fresh coriander (15 g)
- 1 lime
- extra virgin olive oil
- 1 large free-range egg
- 1 mug self-raising flour
- 1 mug semi-skimmed milk
- olive oil
- 300 g cottage cheese
- hot chilli sauce

## Instructions
1. Halve, destone, peel and finely slice the avocado and quarter the tomatoes, then place in a salad bowl with a quarter of the spinach.
2. Trim, finely slice and add the spring onions and pick in the coriander leaves, then squeeze over the lime juice.
3. Drizzle with 1 tablespoon of extra virgin olive oil, season to perfection with sea salt and black pepper, toss to coat and put aside.
4. Crack the egg into a blender, add the flour, milk, remaining spinach and a pinch of salt and pepper, then blitz until smooth.
5. Place a large non-stick frying pan on a medium heat, rub the pan with a little olive oil, then pour in a thin layer of batter, swirling it up and around the edges.
6. Cook on one side only for 2 minutes, or until lightly golden, then stack up on a serving plate and repeat.
7. Top each pancake with dollops of cottage cheese, the avocado salad, and a few good shakes of chilli sauce. Really nice served with extra lime wedges for squeezing over, and a fried egg on top, if you fancy.

## Notes
Colourful, healthy and fun — works just as well for dinner as a weekend brunch. Save any leftover batter in a covered bowl in the fridge for a breakfast treat later in the week.

**Easy swaps:** instead of spinach you could use watercress, rocket, kale, or a mixture of soft herbs such as parsley, mint, basil or tarragon. Cottage cheese can be swapped for mozzarella, halloumi, or crumbled feta.
