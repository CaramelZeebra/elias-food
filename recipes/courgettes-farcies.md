## Ingredients
- 2 onions, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp oil
- 150 g sausage meat
- 400 g minced beef
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- 2 tbsp chopped parsley
- 2 tbsp hot water
- tomatoes (to stuff)
- 50 g grated gruyère
- 2 tbsp olive oil

## Instructions
1. Peel and very finely chop the onions; brown them in 2 tbsp hot oil. Add the sausage meat and minced beef; sauté, breaking it up with a fork. Add the garlic, tomato purée and chopped parsley. Moisten with the hot water. Leave to cook for 1 hour over a very low heat, stirring frequently.
2. Cut the tomatoes and scoop them out lightly, finely chopping the removed flesh. Add it to the previous mixture 10 minutes before the end of cooking. Adjust the seasoning, remove from the heat, and stir in 50g of grated gruyère.
3. Fill the tomatoes with the stuffing. Oil a gratin dish and place the tomatoes in it; sprinkle with 50g of grated gruyère; drizzle with 2 tbsp olive oil. Bake for 25 minutes in a hot oven.

## Notes
Despite the title, this recipe stuffs tomatoes rather than courgettes with a beef and sausage meat mixture. A simple, homely French family recipe.
