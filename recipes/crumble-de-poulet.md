## Ingredients
- 3 chicken escalopes
- 1 small aubergine (eggplant)
- 3 tomatoes
- 1 apple
- 1 tbsp curry powder
- 10 cl coconut milk
- 1 tbsp desiccated coconut
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 100 g flour
- 100 g soft butter
- 50 g sugar (less is better — 30g worked well)
- salt, pepper

## Instructions
1. Peel the apple. Cut it into small cubes along with the aubergine and tomatoes. Cut the chicken escalopes into strips.
2. Heat the olive oil in a pan and cook the aubergine down.
3. After 10 minutes, add the chicken, tomato, and apple.
4. Sprinkle with curry powder and desiccated coconut. Season with salt and pepper. Cook for another 10 minutes.
5. At the end of cooking, add the coconut milk and stir.
6. Pour the mixture into a gratin dish.
7. In a bowl, make the crumble topping by rubbing the flour, soft butter, and sugar together with your fingertips until sandy in texture.
8. Crumble the topping over the mixture and sprinkle with curry powder and desiccated coconut.
9. Bake the crumble for 25 minutes in an oven preheated to 200°C (gas mark 6/7).
