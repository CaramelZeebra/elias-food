## Ingredients
- 15 g fresh ginger
- 1/2 chilli
- 1 aubergine
- 1.5 tbsp oil
- 3 tbsp tamari (or soy sauce)
- 5 tbsp water
- 150 g tinned chickpeas
- 250 g oyster mushrooms
- 2 tbsp curry powder
- 1 small bunch fresh coriander/basil/chives
- 160 g tinned coconut milk
- 400 g tinned tomatoes
- 1/2 lemon, juiced
- 1 tsp black pepper

## Instructions
1. Peel and finely chop the ginger. Remove the end off the chilli (and if you don't like it spicy, remove the seeds too) and finely chop.
2. Finely chop the aubergine into small pieces (a serrated knife works well). Finely chop the oyster mushrooms.
3. Put a large saucepan on a high heat and add the oil, leaving it to heat up for a minute. Add the chilli and ginger and cook for 2 minutes, stirring regularly. Add the oyster mushrooms and cook for 3-4 minutes.
4. Add the aubergine and cook for 2 minutes, stirring regularly. Add the tamari and cook for a further 2 minutes, continuing to stir well so the aubergine and mushrooms get infused with the tamari flavour. Add the 5 tablespoons of water and cook, stirring regularly, until the aubergine becomes soft and cooked.
5. Meanwhile, drain and rinse the chickpeas thoroughly. Roughly chop the coriander/basil/chives and set aside.
6. Add all the remaining ingredients into the pan and cook for 5 minutes; once it boils, reduce to a simmer.
7. Remove from the heat, taste and adjust the seasoning. Garnish each serving with chopped coriander/basil/chives — additional chilli flakes on top if you like it spicy.

## Notes
From The Happy Pear's online Happy Gut Course. Suitable for a sensitive stomach; goes great with toasted gluten-free pittas chopped into soldiers, or a side of brown rice. Keyword: low fodmap, plantbased, vegan.
