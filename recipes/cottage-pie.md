## Ingredients
- 1 onion
- 1 carrot
- 1 tbsp oil
- 500 g beef mince
- 2 tbsp flour
- 1 tbsp tomato sauce (ketchup)
- 1/2 to 1 tbsp Worcester sauce
- 1/2 cup peas
- 1/2 cup corn kernels (optional)
- 1 bouquet garni or a small bunch of herbs chopped finely, plus 1 whole bay leaf
- 3/4 to 1 cup beef stock
- 3-4 large potatoes
- 1 tbsp butter
- salt and pepper

## Instructions
1. Peel and chop the onion and carrot finely. Heat the oil in a large frypan or saucepan, add the onion and cook until clear or translucent. Add the minced beef and sauté, using a wooden spoon to break up the mince so it is nice and fine. When the mince is thoroughly browned, stir through the flour and cook for one minute. Add the tomato sauce, Worcester sauce, finely chopped carrot, peas, corn and herbs. Stir thoroughly to combine. Gradually add the stock and bring to the boil. Reduce the heat and cover, simmering for 10 to 15 minutes. Add salt and pepper to taste — the mixture should be quite thick. Remove and discard the bouquet garni or whole bay leaf and set the mixture aside.
2. Peel the potatoes, chop into quarters and place in a medium saucepan with cold water just covering the potatoes. Add a little salt to the water and bring to the boil. Reduce the heat and simmer for 10 to 15 minutes or until the potatoes are tender. Drain and place back on the heat for a few seconds to let any extra water evaporate. Mash the potatoes with one tablespoon of butter until very creamy — add a little milk if necessary. Add salt to taste.
3. Preheat the oven to 190°C, 375°F or gas mark 5. Spoon the mince mixture into a pie dish, then spoon or pipe the creamy mashed potato over the mince. Sprinkle grated cheese on top if desired and bake for 15 to 20 minutes or until the potato is golden and the pie is heated through.

## Notes
Serves 4. Cottage Pie and Shepherd's Pie are very similar — Shepherd's Pie is made from lamb whereas Cottage Pie uses beef. This version uses fresh minced beef, though leftover roast beef also works well.
