## Ingredients
- 1 pork filet mignon (tenderloin)
- 4-5 courgettes, cut into chunks
- 1 onion, sliced
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 good handful raisins
- salt, pepper, herbes de Provence
- 200 ml chicken stock
- 1 tin chickpeas
- couscous, to serve

## Instructions
1. Brown the pork filet mignon in a sauté pan.
2. Wash and cut the courgettes into chunks.
3. Add the courgettes to the pan along with the sliced onion, crushed garlic and a good handful of raisins.
4. Season with salt, pepper and herbes de Provence.
5. Pour in the chicken stock, cover, and cook over a low heat for 25 minutes.
6. 5 minutes before the end, add the chickpeas and prepare the couscous according to packet instructions.
7. Ready to serve!

## Notes
A quick, informal weeknight couscous — sweet from the raisins, savoury from the pork and stock.
