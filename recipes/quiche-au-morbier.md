## Ingredients
- 250 g shortcrust pastry
- 350 g morbier cheese
- 3 williams pears
- 250 g sugar
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- 1/4 bunch fresh mint
- 10 g butter
- 1 l water (for poaching the pears)
- 4 eggs
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp pepper
- 1 pinch freshly grated nutmeg
- 35 cl crème fleurette (light pouring cream)

## Instructions
1. Make the quiche filling: whisk the eggs with the salt, pepper, and freshly grated nutmeg, then add the cream and mix well.
2. Preheat the oven to 210°C (gas mark 7).
3. Peel the pears. Bring the water, sugar, and lemon juice to the boil, then poach the whole pears in it over low heat for 1 hour.
4. Butter a deep-sided tin about 21cm in diameter. Roll out the pastry and line the tin.
5. Once the pears are poached, drain them, cut them into quarters, and remove the cores. Arrange them in a rosette on the pastry.
6. Remove the rind from the morbier and cut it into 1/2cm cubes. Place the cubes between the pears.
7. Wash and finely chop the mint, and scatter it over the cheese.
8. Pour in the quiche filling and adjust the seasoning.
9. Bake for 30 minutes. Serve hot.

## Notes
You can use tinned or jarred pears in syrup instead — in that case, skip poaching them.
