## Ingredients
- 3 eggs
- 150 g flour
- 1 sachet baking powder
- 100 ml sunflower oil
- 125 ml whole milk
- 100 g grated gruyère
- 200 g fresh tuna
- 500 g green pepper
- 500 g red pepper
- olive oil
- 2 pinches salt
- 2 pinches pepper

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (gas mark 6).
2. Grill the peppers, peel them, deseed, and cut into thin strips. Gently fry them in a little olive oil for about 20 minutes. Add the tuna, cut into chunks, along with the salt and pepper, then sauté for 5 minutes.
3. In a mixing bowl, whisk together the eggs, flour and baking powder. Gradually incorporate the oil and the pre-warmed milk. Add the grated gruyère. Fold in the tuna and pepper mixture.
4. Pour into an ungreased loaf tin and bake for 45 minutes.

## Notes
**Variations:**

**Tuna and tomato:** peel 2 large tomatoes, deseed and pan-fry for 15 minutes with a little oil; finely chop 6 basil leaves and add to the base.

**Tuna and chives:** replace the peppers with 5 sprigs of finely chopped chives.
