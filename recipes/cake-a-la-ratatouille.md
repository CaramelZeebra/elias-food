## Ingredients
- 3 eggs
- 150 g plain flour
- 1 sachet baking powder
- 80 ml sunflower oil
- 125 ml whole milk
- 100 g grated gruyère
- 1 tomato
- 1/2 onion
- 1/2 aubergine
- 1/2 courgette
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 3 basil leaves
- 1 small bunch chervil
- 2 pinches salt
- 2 pinches pepper

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (gas mark 6).
2. Peel and chop all the vegetables. In a frying pan, heat 2 tbsp olive oil and lightly brown the onion, then add the tomato, aubergine, courgette, salt and pepper. Leave to simmer for about 20 minutes.
3. Leave to cool, then scatter over the chopped basil and chervil.
4. Meanwhile, whisk together the eggs, flour and baking powder. Gradually incorporate the oil and the pre-warmed milk. Add the gruyère and mix. Fold the vegetables into the batter and mix.
5. Pour into an ungreased loaf tin and bake for 45 minutes.

## Notes
A French savoury loaf cake studded with a mini ratatouille of aubergine, courgette and tomato. Nice served in slices as a starter or with drinks.
