## Ingredients
- 1 semi-boned lamb shoulder (about 1.6 kg)
- 1 onion
- 6 carrots
- 2 leeks
- 2 courgettes
- 1 aubergine
- 1 bell pepper
- 1 clove garlic
- 1/2 tin chopped tomatoes
- 1 tin tomato purée
- 1 stick celery
- chickpeas
- couscous (semolina), to serve
- butter
- salt, pepper

## Instructions
1. In a large pot, brown the lamb shoulder in butter.
2. Add the onion. Season with salt and pepper. Cover with water.
3. Add the carrots, leeks, courgettes, aubergine, pepper, garlic, chopped tomatoes, tomato purée, and celery stick.
4. Cook for 3 to 4 hours.
5. Add the chickpeas 30 minutes before the end of cooking.
6. Serve with couscous (semolina).
