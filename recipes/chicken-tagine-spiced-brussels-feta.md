## Notes
Chicken thighs simmered with cinnamon, turmeric and smoked paprika, dried apricots and chickpeas, topped with cumin-fried shredded Brussels sprouts and crumbled feta. A Joe Wicks recipe. Recipe from Good Food magazine, November 2016. Full ingredients and method at the source link above.
