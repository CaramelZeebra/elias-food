## Ingredients
- 6-8 slices lamb collar/neck
- 500 g potatoes
- 3 carrots
- 3 onions
- 2 garlic cloves
- 5 sprigs mint
- 1 lemon
- 250 g wheat (blé)
- 400 ml beef stock
- 2 tbsp honey
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 star anise
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp ground ginger
- salt, pepper

## Instructions
1. Peel and finely chop the garlic and onions. Peel the carrots and cut into chunks. Heat the olive oil in a cocotte and brown the lamb slices on all sides.
2. Lower the heat, add the prepared vegetables, the spices, salt and pepper, then pour in the stock. Cover and leave to simmer for 50 minutes.
3. Add the peeled and chopped potatoes, the wheat, the honey and the lemon juice to the cocotte. Cover again and cook very gently for a further 30 minutes.
4. Serve either straight from the cocotte or in a tagine dish, scattered with chopped mint.

## Notes
For 6 people. A warming Moroccan-inspired lamb dish built around collar of lamb, root vegetables and wheat, sweetened with honey and lifted with lemon and mint.
