## Notes
A meat-free take on shepherd's pie: a rich puy lentil, carrot and mushroom base under a buttery mashed potato topping, finished with cheddar. Recipe from Good Food magazine, April 2009. Freezes well for up to two months — full ingredients, method, and slow cooker/freezing tips at the source link above.
