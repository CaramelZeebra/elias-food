## Notes
Shallots and butternut squash simmered with cinnamon, ginger, prunes and honey, then finished with peppers and served over harissa-spiced chickpea couscous with toasted almonds. Recipe from Good Food magazine, May 2007. Full ingredients and method at the source link above.
