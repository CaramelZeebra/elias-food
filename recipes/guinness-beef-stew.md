## Notes
Diced rumsteak braised with onions, garlic and carrots in a Guinness and beef stock gravy, thickened with flour, with a handful of pitted prunes stirred in near the end for a touch of sweetness against the beer's bitterness. Finished with parsley. Full ingredients and method (in French) at the source link above.
