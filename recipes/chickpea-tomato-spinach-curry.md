## Notes
A low-calorie, vegan curry (from BBC Good Food's fasting-day collection) — onion, garlic, ginger and tomato blitzed to a purée, cooked down with spices, yeast extract, red lentils and coconut cream, then finished with broccoli, chickpeas, spinach and a squeeze of lemon. Serve with brown rice.
