## Notes
A quick curry base of onion, curry powder, ginger and garlic simmered with tomatoes and chickpeas, topped with cod fillets and steamed through, finished with lemon and coriander. Recipe from Good Food magazine, September 2016. Full ingredients and method at the source link above.
