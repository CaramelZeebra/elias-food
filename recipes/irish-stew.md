## Ingredients
- 8 lamb chops
- 4 slices streaky bacon (rashers)
- 1 kg potatoes, cut into thick slices
- 3 large carrots, cut into diagonal chunks
- 3 large onions, cut into thick rounds
- 500 ml stock (use more if you like a soupier stew — enough to cover)
- pepper, thyme, chopped parsley

## Instructions
1. Trim excess fat from the lamb chops. Cut the bacon into strips.
2. Fry the bacon in a little fat (oil, butter, etc.) and set aside.
3. Brown the chops on each side and set aside.
4. In a casserole dish, layer half the potatoes, carrots and onions in the bottom. Season with pepper. Add half the bacon.
5. Add the lamb on top, then cover with the rest of the vegetables. Season with pepper.
6. Pour in the stock and sprinkle with thyme.
7. Cover and bring to the boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for at least 1 hour.
8. Serve sprinkled with parsley.

## Notes
As with all stews, this tastes even better the next day. Avoid stirring too much so the vegetables don't break down into mush.
