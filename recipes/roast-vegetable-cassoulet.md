## Notes
Slow-cooked haricot beans in a tarragon tomato sauce with roasted squash and celeriac, finished with a herby breadcrumb topping. Recipe from Good Food magazine, March 2002. Can be assembled a day ahead. Full ingredients and method at the source link above.
