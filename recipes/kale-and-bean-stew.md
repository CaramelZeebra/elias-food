## Ingredients
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 4 shallots, chopped
- 1 carrot, chopped
- 1 celery stalk, trimmed and chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 250 ml dry white wine
- 3 sprigs thyme
- 1 bay leaf
- 800 ml to 1 l chicken or vegetable stock
- 800 g tinned white beans, such as cannellini, butter or haricot (2 x 400g cans), drained and rinsed
- 500 g kale, shredded, stems and centre ribs discarded
- 1 tbsp sherry vinegar (optional)
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped, to serve

## Instructions
1. Warm the olive oil in a large saucepan over medium heat. Tip in the shallots, carrot, celery and garlic, season with salt and pepper, and cook for 10-12 minutes, stirring now and then, until everything is soft and lightly golden.
2. Pour in the wine and let it bubble for around 5 minutes until slightly reduced. Drop in the thyme and bay leaf, then add most of the stock (save a splash back). Bring up to a boil, then turn the heat down low and let it simmer for about 15 minutes.
3. Tip in the drained beans and let everything simmer together for a few more minutes to heat through.
4. Stir through the shredded kale and cook for 3-5 minutes more, just until it wilts and softens. Fish out the thyme sprigs and bay leaf. Loosen with the reserved stock if you'd like it a bit thinner.
5. Finish with the sherry vinegar if using, taste and adjust the seasoning, then ladle into bowls and scatter over the parsley to serve.

## Notes
A simple, warming one-pot loosely based on a Rachel Allen stew — good without the sherry vinegar too, if you'd rather leave it out.
